Onboard Hub

Operations · SaaS

An onboarding management system connecting bookings, migrations, exports, and operational handoffs.

Open product ↗View repository ↗
Product snapshot

The problem: A booking is only the beginning. Migrations, exports and handoffs can leave teams working from different versions of the same story.

The product: One connected onboarding flow that makes the next action and operational handoff easier to follow.

Demo Hub

Pre-sales · SaaS

A pre-sales booking tool with lead capture, calendar scheduling, rescheduling, and a shared team dashboard.

Open product ↗View repository ↗
Product snapshot

The problem: A promising lead can lose momentum in the back-and-forth of booking, rescheduling and coordinating with a sales team.

The product: A connected path from captured interest to a scheduled conversation, with a dashboard for the team.

Resume outcome: 1,500 demos booked and 200 converted to paid customers.
